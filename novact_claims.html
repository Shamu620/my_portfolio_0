Overview

One place to track claims across three categories.

The portal brings Motor, Non-Motor and Credit Life claims into a single system. Staff can see every claim with its client, status, category and underwriter, search by client, filter by status and open a detail dialog for the category they are working in.

I built it with Django to learn how a real backend application fits together: models, views, templates and forms working as one system. Next I plan to rebuild it in Go, focusing on faster request handling and better performance as the volume of claims grows.

Features

What the Django version does.

  • Reports and Analysis area with three views: Underwriter Performance, Claims Data and Trends.
  • Claims table showing client, status, category and underwriter for every claim.
  • Search by client, a status filter, sortable columns and a selectable number of entries per page.
  • Read-and-write claim detail dialogs for each claim category.
